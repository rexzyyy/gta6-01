Neon Palms: Vice Coast is an original top-down open-world crime game. Explore a sun-soaked city on foot or by car, steal cars, outrun the police as your wanted level rises, and run six story missions for Luz.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser. Keyboard: WASD to walk or drive, E to enter or steal a car, F to take a job, mouse click to shoot, Esc to pause, M to mute.
